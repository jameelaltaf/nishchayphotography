# Newborn session — client preparation guidelines

Transcribed from the studio's own Canva document (all 9 points, complete).
Written in Nishchay's voice, first person.

**Use:** booking confirmation email, and/or a "how to prepare" page linked from
the booking flow. This is not marketing copy — it is what a booked client needs
to read before they arrive.

---

Hello,

I'm so excited to welcome you and your newborn to Nish Impressions Studio for a
beautiful photoshoot! To make sure we have a smooth and successful session, here
are a few important things to keep in mind:

1. **Timing:** It's best to schedule the photoshoot when your baby is between
   **5–20 days old**. During this time, they tend to be sleepier and easier to
   pose, resulting in those perfect, cozy newborn shots.

2. **Feeding:** If possible, please feed your baby before you arrive at the
   studio. A well-fed baby is typically more content and sleepy, which helps
   create a calm environment for the shoot.

3. **Studio Temperature:** The studio will be kept quite warm to ensure your baby
   stays comfortable, especially if we do any shots without clothing. You might
   want to dress lightly to stay comfortable in the warm environment.

4. **Outfits and Props:** I will have a range of outfits, blankets, and props
   ready for you to choose from. However, feel free to bring any special items,
   outfits, or personal heirlooms you'd like to include in the photos. Personal
   touches can make the photos even more meaningful.

5. **Sleep Preparation:** If possible, try to keep your baby awake for a little
   while before you arrive. This can help them fall asleep more easily once we
   begin, allowing for those serene sleeping poses.

6. **Pacifier:** If your baby uses a pacifier, please bring it along. Even if
   they don't typically use one, it can be helpful to soothe them during the
   session if they get fussy.

7. **Patience:** Newborn sessions can take time due to feeding, changing, and
   settling the baby. Please plan for the session to last a few hours, and don't
   worry — we'll take breaks as needed to keep everyone comfortable.

8. **Health and Safety:** Your baby's safety is my highest priority. I follow all
   necessary cleanliness and handling protocols to ensure a safe environment and
   careful handling throughout the session.

9. **Special Items:** If you have any sentimental blankets, toys, or family
   heirlooms you'd like to incorporate, please bring them along. These items can
   add a personal touch to the photos and make them even more memorable.

I look forward to capturing these precious moments for you and your family. If
you have any questions or need to adjust the timing, please feel free to reach
out.

See you soon!

Warm regards,
**Nishchay**
*Photographer at Nish Impressions Studio*
