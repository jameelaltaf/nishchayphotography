Legal

Privacy notice

Last updated: 1 August 2026. This notice is written to reflect Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). Template text — have it reviewed by a Canadian lawyer before publishing.

Who we are

Nish Impressions Studio is a photography studio operating in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. For questions about this notice or about your personal information, contact hello@nishchayphotography.ca.

What we collect

  • Enquiry details — the name, email, phone number, event date, venue, guest count, budget range and message you submit through our contact form or by email.
  • Client records — contract details, invoicing and payment records, and the correspondence relating to your booking.
  • Photographs — the images we create for you, which are personal information about the people in them.
  • Website analytics — if analytics are enabled, aggregated and de-identified data about how pages are used. We do not use advertising trackers or sell any data.

Why we collect it

To answer your enquiry, prepare a quote, fulfil a contract, deliver galleries and albums, meet Canada Revenue Agency record-keeping obligations, and — only where you have separately opted in — send occasional planning emails.

Consent

We collect personal information with your knowledge and consent. Ticking the consent box on our contact form is consent for us to store your enquiry and reply to it. Marketing emails require a separate opt-in and every one contains a one-click unsubscribe. Withdrawing consent for marketing does not affect our ability to service an existing booking.

Use of your photographs

Our standard contract requests permission to show a selection of your images in our portfolio, journal and social media. This permission is optional and can be declined without affecting your price, your coverage or your gallery. If you decline, or withdraw permission later in writing, we remove the images from anything we control. Images already printed in third-party publications cannot be recalled.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't become bookings: deleted after 24 months.
  • Contracts and financial records: retained for 7 years, as required for Canadian tax purposes.
  • Delivered galleries: online for 12 months to 3 years depending on your collection.
  • Our working archive of your images: 2 years after delivery, then deleted.

Who else sees it

We use third-party service providers to run the studio — a gallery and print platform, an email provider, a payment processor and an accounting service. Each receives only what it needs. Some store data outside Canada, principally in the United States, which means that data may be subject to the laws of that country. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information to anyone.

How we protect it

Encrypted storage and transfer, two-factor authentication on every studio account, access limited to the three people who work here, and encrypted offsite backups. No system is perfect; if a breach ever creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as PIPEDA requires.

Your rights

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete what we're not legally required to keep, and withdraw consent for marketing at any time. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Cookies

This website sets no advertising or tracking cookies. If analytics are enabled they are configured to anonymise IP addresses. Our gallery platform sets its own cookies when you visit a gallery; see that provider's privacy policy.
