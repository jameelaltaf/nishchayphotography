Our commitment

Nish Impressions Studio is committed to providing an accessible website and accessible service to everyone, including people with disabilities. This site is built to target WCAG 2.1 Level AA, the standard referenced by Ontario's Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA) and by the federal Accessible Canada Act.

What we've built in

  • Semantic HTML with a logical heading structure on every page
  • A skip link to main content, and a visible focus indicator on every interactive element
  • Full keyboard operation — navigation, gallery filters, image viewer and forms all work without a mouse
  • Descriptive alternative text on photographs, and decorative images marked as such
  • Text and interface colours tested against WCAG AA contrast ratios
  • Form fields with persistent labels, clear error messages and correct autocomplete attributes
  • Animation that respects the operating system's reduce motion setting
  • Layouts that reflow without horizontal scrolling down to 320 px and up to 200% text zoom

Known limitations

Our client gallery platform is a third-party service and its accessibility is outside our direct control. If you have difficulty accessing your gallery, contact us and we will deliver your images by another method at no cost.

Accessible service

Our Mississauga studio is accessible by elevator. Tell us in advance about any accommodation that would make a session or a meeting work better for you — seating, breaks, lighting sensitivity, a support person, a service animal, or a specific communication method — and we will arrange it.

Tell us when something doesn't work

Accessibility feedback goes straight to the studio, and we treat it as a bug report rather than a suggestion. Email hello@nishchayphotography.ca or call 514-649-7865. We acknowledge feedback within two business days and will tell you what we're doing about it. Feedback can be given in whatever format is easiest for you.
