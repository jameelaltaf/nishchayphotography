The studio

We photograph the parts of the day nobody thinks to pose for.

Nish Impressions Studio is a small Toronto studio built on a simple idea: the pictures you'll still love in twenty years are rarely the ones you asked for. Our work sits between reportage and editorial — we stay out of the way through the moments that matter, then step in with real direction for the portraits that need it.

We shoot across the GTA and all of Ontario — from Distillery District ceremonies to Muskoka lakefronts and Niagara vineyards — and we travel for the days worth travelling for.

How it works

About

Eleven years, one stubborn habit.

Nishchay started photographing weddings in 2015 with a borrowed camera and an unhelpful amount of opinion about flash. The opinions have softened; the habit hasn't — watching the room instead of directing it, and only stepping in when a picture genuinely needs a hand.

Today the studio is a team of three: two photographers and an editor who guards the six-week delivery promise like it's a personal grudge.
