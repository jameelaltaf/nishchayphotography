What's in there

  • Full-resolution downloads — every edited image, in web and print sizes
  • Favourites & proofing — star the images you want in an album and share the list with us
  • Print store — professional prints, framing and wall art, shipped across Canada
  • Album proofing — review your layout and request changes in the gallery itself
  • Sharing — send the gallery to family with its own password, or a single image at a time

How long galleries stay online

Wedding galleries stay live for three years on The Full Day and The Multi-Event collections, and twelve months on The Intimate and on session work. Download your images and back them up in two places as soon as you get them — an online gallery is a delivery method, not an archive. We keep our own backup for two years, but we can't promise recovery beyond that.

Print ordering

Prints ordered through the gallery come from a professional Canadian lab and are colour-managed against our edit. Prices are in CAD and include shipping within Canada. Ordering elsewhere is entirely your call — your print release allows it — but consumer labs will shift the colour, and we can't fix that from here.
