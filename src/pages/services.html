What moves the number

Three things, mostly: how many hours you need, whether the day needs two photographers to be covered honestly, and how far we're travelling. Guest count matters less than most people expect — a 200-guest wedding in one venue is often simpler than 60 guests across three locations.

Saturdays in June, September and October book 12–18 months out. Friday, Sunday and off-season dates are frequently available at shorter notice, and we're happy to say so.
